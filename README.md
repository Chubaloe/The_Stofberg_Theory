# The Stofberg Theory

**An Open Scientific Hypothesis Exploring the Fundamental Nature of the Universe**

**Original concept and hypothesis by Thom Stofberg**

Welcome to the official GitHub repository of **The Stofberg Theory**.

The Stofberg Theory is an exploratory physical hypothesis proposing that all physical phenomena may emerge from the motion and interactions of two fundamental constituents: North and South magnetic monopoles.

The goal of this project is to investigate whether a single, consistent set of interaction laws could explain the emergence of matter, magnetic fields, light, electricity, gravity, black holes, and the evolution of the universe.

**This is an unverified scientific hypothesis, not an established physical theory.** Its fundamental assumptions and proposed mechanisms require mathematical development, numerical investigation, and experimental validation.

## The Central Hypothesis

The Stofberg Theory begins with several proposed assumptions:

- The universe consists fundamentally of North and South magnetic monopoles.
- These monopoles have no intrinsic rest mass.
- Every monopole moves continuously at the speed of light.
- Opposite polarities attract, while matching polarities repel.
- Interactions change the directions of monopole motion and depend on distance and angular relationships.
- Stable chaotic configurations may form what we observe as matter.
- Stable organized flows may form what we observe as magnetic fields.

From these assumptions, the theory explores possible explanations for other physical phenomena.

Whether such structures and behaviors actually emerge from the proposed interaction laws remains an open research question.

## Research Objectives

The primary objective is to determine whether the fundamental assumptions produce physically meaningful and mathematically consistent results.

Important research questions include:

- Can the proposed monopole interaction laws be expressed in consistent mathematical equations?
- Can stable chaotic structures emerge naturally from interacting monopoles?
- Can stable organized monopole flows exist?
- What determines the stability and properties of these structures?
- Can any resulting configurations reproduce the observed properties of known particles?
- Can the proposed dynamics reproduce established electromagnetic, gravitational, and relativistic phenomena?
- Can the proposed cosmological mechanisms be tested against astronomical observations?

Researchers are encouraged to investigate these questions independently.

Negative results, contradictions, and critical evaluations are just as valuable as supportive findings.

**The purpose of this project is to discover what the assumptions actually predict, not to assume that the theory is correct.**

## Scientific Transparency

The Stofberg Theory is a speculative research proposal.

No claim is made that magnetic monopoles of the proposed type have been experimentally demonstrated, that the proposed interaction laws describe nature, or that the theory has successfully reproduced established physical results.

The project welcomes scrutiny and will distinguish hypotheses, mathematical results, simulation findings, and experimental evidence.
